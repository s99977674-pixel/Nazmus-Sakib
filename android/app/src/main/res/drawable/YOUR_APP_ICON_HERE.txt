========================================================================
APP ICON / LOGO ASSET REPLACEMENT GUIDE
========================================================================

To replace the application icon and branding logo with your own photo or logo:

1. Prepare your custom image:
   - Format: PNG or JPG
   - Recommended resolution: 512x512 pixels or 1024x1024 pixels (Square aspect ratio)

2. Replace the following files with your new image:
   - android/app/src/main/res/drawable/app_logo.png
   - android/app/src/main/res/drawable/logo.png
   - public/YOUR_APP_ICON_HERE.png
   - public/logo.png

3. (Optional) In-App Realtime Upload:
   Inside the running application, click the camera / upload badge on the
   app logo in the header to select your photo directly from your device.
   The app will instantly update your logo across the UI and PDF watermark!

4. Regenerate Android mipmap launcher icons:
   - mipmap-mdpi/ic_launcher.png (48x48)
   - mipmap-hdpi/ic_launcher.png (72x72)
   - mipmap-xhdpi/ic_launcher.png (96x96)
   - mipmap-xxhdpi/ic_launcher.png (144x144)
   - mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png (192x192)
   - drawable/ic_launcher_512.png (512x512)
